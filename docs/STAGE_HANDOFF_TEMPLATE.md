# Feature test stage handoff template

Copy this section into the feature plan or a linked review issue at the end of each stage. Keep the summary short enough for a human to review before reading detailed evidence. A missing answer stays marked **unknown**; do not fill it with an agent assumption.

## Stage [number]: [role] — [feature]

- **Status:** draft / ready for human review / revise / approved / paused
- **Prepared by and date:** [agent or person; date]
- **Inputs reviewed:** [feature request, requirements, repo revision, target evidence, previous handoff]
- **What was done:** [specific artifact changes, case IDs, files, or analysis]
- **What the evidence shows:** [verified observations with links; distinguish inferred or proposed behavior]
- **Choices and reasons:** [choice, simpler or plausible alternative, why this choice fits now]
- **Checks:** [exact commands or review performed and results; say if no tests or live access occurred]
- **Risks and open questions:** [question, owner, effect on the next stage]
- **Skeptical review:** [reviewer, date, findings, disposition, revision rechecked; or pending/not required]
- **Recommended next step:** [one bounded stage and allowed scope]

### Human decision

- **Decision requested from:** [named person or role]
- **Decision requested:** approve this stage and the stated next step / revise / pause
- **Decision:** pending until explicitly recorded
- **Decision maker, date, and reviewed revision:** [complete only after a response]
- **Conditions and actions authorized:** [exact scope; separate product, data, live execution, production, and publication approvals]

The coordinator presents this summary and links to the underlying artifacts before asking for a decision. Record a decision in the plan or linked issue; do not infer one from silence or the presence of this template. If the human requests revisions, update the artifact and present a new summary of what changed before asking again.
